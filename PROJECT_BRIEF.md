# Fly Me to the Moon

## Concept

The player begins on home-base planet **612-B** and jumps between small planets floating through space. When the player gets close enough to a planet, gravity automatically locks onto its surface, allowing the player to move around it and launch toward the next destination.

## Target

- Unity 6.6 (`6000.6.3f1`)
- 2D URP
- Desktop build for macOS/Windows
- Pixel-art presentation
- Single-player

## MVP

- A controllable player on a circular planet
- Forgiving jump and airborne movement
- Automatic gravity lock to a nearby planet
- Respawn after falling into space
- Home planet 612-B and at least two destination planets
- A clear start, route, and win state
- A playable desktop build

## Stretch goals

- More instrument-themed planets
- Distinct musical feedback per planet
- Particles, animation, title screen, and additional polish

## Scope rule

The complete playable route is more important than the number of planets. Do not add stretch work until the MVP route is playable from start to finish.
